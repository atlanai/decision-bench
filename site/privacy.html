Privacy

Decision Bench uses Google Analytics only if you choose “Allow analytics.” It records page views, navigation and download clicks, filter and comparison changes, and interactions with benchmark controls. Events include the page type, public benchmark identifiers (such as task, model, row and dataset), and selected filter metadata. Search is recorded only as active or inactive. We do not send search terms, review notes, imported file contents, record text, or URL query strings. External link events include only the destination domain.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can change your choice through “Analytics preferences” in the site footer. If you withdraw permission after allowing analytics, we stop sending new events from this browser; previously collected data remains subject to Google Analytics retention settings.
